# Plumbline roadmap

Nothing below may delay the January 5, 2027 launch or the App Store submission.

## 1. Now: church planting couples
- Paid launch January 5, 2027: $99 per couple, $2,500 a year unlimited, founding networks $79 per couple for life.
- November beta: 5 assessments for each beta network.
- iPhone app submitted to the App Store.

## 2. Next, after launch: individual mode
- A single candidate: no spouse, no couple report, "candidate" wording instead of "planter".
- First version is church staff hiring (for example a worship pastor or student pastor), with questions fitted to staff roles in place of planting questions.

## 3. Later: nonprofits and business
- Leadership and hiring assessments for nonprofits, then businesses.
- Faith-specific sections (calling, spiritual gifts, fivefold) would be optional or swapped out.

## Rules for other assessments (all versions)
Plumbline lets people type in results from Working Genius, DISC, Myers-Briggs, CliftonStrengths, Enneagram and Predictive Index. It never uses their questions, report text, definitions or graphics, and it shows each one's trademark and non-affiliation line. Those results are shown next to Plumbline's own results but never scored. Working Genius only adds gap flags and interview questions.
