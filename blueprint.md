# Planter Assessment — Blueprint v0.1

Working name: TBD (options in brand step). Built by Parker Manuel. Free for any network.

## Who takes what
| Person | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| Planter | ~30 min | Seven pillars, Wiring, Gifts, Fivefold, Scenarios, Couple (paired), Story (experience facts) |
| Spouse | ~20 min | Her own calling, health, gifts, wiring; Couple (paired + guess-your-spouse); private note to leader |
| References (3–5) | ~8 min | Observable behaviors, gifts seen, "Follow Test", open text |
| Network leader | — | Creates the case, sends links, gets the Leader Report + Interview Guide |

Rules: each person has their own link; answers are sealed until everyone finishes; couple is asked not to discuss answers. Single planters skip Couple sections.

## The seven pillars (planter)
Each pillar = 2–4 scales × 4–6 items (Likert 1–5, ~25% reverse-keyed).
1. **Calling & Faith** — clarity of call, confirmed by others, devotional life, risk-taking faith
2. **Character** — integrity, humility/teachability, ownership of mistakes
3. **Emotional Health** — self-awareness, resilience, rhythms & Sabbath, current load/burnout signs
4. **Marriage & Family** — partnership, family first, couple alignment (paired)
5. **Leadership** — vision casting, building teams & ownership, developing leaders, conflict
6. **Mission** — relating to the unchurched, personal evangelism, making disciples, reading the community
7. **Planting Capacity** — self-starter drive, adaptability, money & fundraising, communication/preaching, systems

Plus **Readiness Facts** (scored by rubric, not Likert): education, training, years in ministry, roles held, largest team led, people personally led to Christ (12 mo), raised money before, coach in place, sending church, debt level, timeline.

## Profiles (described, never graded)
- **Wiring** — 5 behavioral dimensions (own items, IPIP-informed): Pace (steady↔driving), People (reflective↔social), Drive (collaborative↔independent), Structure (flexible↔precise), Decisions (intuitive↔data). Shown for both spouses side by side.
- **Spiritual Gifts** — 20 biblical gifts (Rom 12, 1 Cor 12, Eph 4, 1 Pet 4 + hospitality, intercession, craftsmanship). Fan chart. Network can hide sign gifts.
- **Fivefold** — Apostolic, Prophetic, Evangelistic, Shepherding, Teaching (forced-choice block). Team-gap advice.

## Scenarios (judgment)
8 planting situations × 4 responses; pick best and worst. Keyed by experienced planters (v1 keyed by Parker; panel later).

## Couple Alignment ("Unity Map")
Paired items both answer alone, in 8 areas: Calling, Vision for the city, Money, Roles, Time & rhythms, Home & kids, Conflict, Support.
- Agreed strength / Gap (2+ apart or opposite sides) / Shared concern.
- Guess-your-spouse items: "How will your spouse answer…?" → "Know each other" score.
- Alignment per area shown in bands.

## Validity
- Candor scale (6 items: impossible virtues / universal faults) → "answers may be idealized".
- 5 consistency pairs, 2 gentle bogus items, page timing → Response Quality: Good / Some concerns / Retake.
- Spouse safety screen + private note: leader-only, never in any couple/planter report. Risk items show help on screen immediately.

## 360 references
Categories: sending/supervising pastor, ministry peer, someone they've led, someone outside the church. "Not observed" on every item. Self-vs-others: blind spot (self higher by a band+), hidden strength (others higher). Group averages shown only with 2+ raters (3+ when anonymous).
Follow Test: "If they planted in your city, how likely would you be to join the launch team?"

## Scoring
- Scale score = mean of items (reversed where keyed) → 0–100.
- Bands: **Strength** ≥ 80 · **Solid** 65–79 · **Developing** 50–64 · **Growth Area** < 50.
- Pillar grade = mean of scales, same bands; letter shown for leader (A/B/C/D) per pillar.
- **Concern flags** (separate from bands): specific items/patterns (e.g., spouse not called, burnout signs high, integrity item, debt high, couple shared concern ≥2 areas, references low Follow Test).
- **Readiness signal** (guidance only): Ready · Ready with a growth plan · Not yet · Talk first (any red concern).
- Cut scores provisional (set by design), reviewed by a panel of planters/assessors after 50–100 cases (modified Angoff). Shown in the "How this was scored" appendix.

## Reports
**Planter/couple report** (encouraging, visual): Wiring for both, gifts fan, fivefold, pillar strengths, growth areas framed as next steps, Unity Map by area (not raw answers), 90-day growth plan, resources.
**Leader report** (clean, graded): "one input, not a decision" banner; readiness signal; response quality; pillar grades; strengths / growth areas / concerns per pillar; blind spots & hidden strengths; couple alignment item-level gaps; spouse private notes; reference summary & Follow Test; experience facts; interview guide (60-min agenda, STAR questions per flag, 1–5 rubric to score live); "How this was scored".

## Theology
Broad evangelical core: Bible's authority, Nicene Creed, salvation by grace through faith, Great Commission. Network can add its own statement and questions later.

## Legal
All items original. Concepts only from public research (Ridley's 13 characteristics, IPIP public domain, biblical gift lists). No CliftonStrengths/PI/Enneagram/MBTI/DiSC/PREPARE names or items. Non-clinical disclaimer. Consent per person.
